# Requirements Document

## Introduction

The Hospital Health Management System (HHMS) is a multi-tenant, cloud-based platform that enables multiple hospitals to operate independently within a single system. Each hospital has its own isolated environment with department-level dashboards, a Flutter-based mobile app for clinical staff, and web-based dashboards for administrative roles. The system supports the full patient journey — from front-desk registration (capturing demographics, chief complaint, and insurance/HMO information) through triage/nursing, doctor consultation, and onward to pharmacy, laboratory, ward/inpatient admission, or appointment booking — culminating in discharge. A Super Admin oversees the entire platform.

The backend is built on **PHP Laravel** (REST API), chosen for its mature ecosystem, rapid development, and strong multi-tenancy support via packages such as `stancl/tenancy`. Web dashboards are served from the same Laravel application using **Vue.js 3 + Inertia.js**, providing a seamless SPA experience without a separate frontend deployment. The Flutter mobile app serves unit-level clinical staff. A dedicated API gateway layer ensures scalability, and a queue-based notification system (Laravel Queues + Redis) handles real-time patient workflow events.

Patient files and photographs are stored in **Cloudflare R2** (an S3-compatible object storage service), chosen for its cost-effective egress pricing and tenant-isolated bucket support. Push notifications to mobile devices are delivered via **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)**, which serves as the fallback delivery channel when the Mobile_App is backgrounded or the device is offline.

Supported department types include, but are not limited to: Front Desk, Triage/Nurse, Doctor, Pharmacy, Laboratory, **Ward/Admission**, and **ICU/A&E**. Hospital Admins may configure any combination of these department types when building their patient journey workflow.

## Glossary

- **HHMS**: Hospital Health Management System — the overall platform described in this document.
- **Super_Admin**: The platform-level administrator who manages all hospitals and controls the entire system.
- **Hospital_Admin**: An administrator scoped to a single hospital who manages that hospital's configuration, staff, and departments.
- **Department**: A clinical or administrative unit within a hospital (e.g., Cardiology, Pharmacy, Laboratory, Front Desk, Ward/Admission, ICU/A&E).
- **Department_Dashboard**: A web or mobile interface scoped to a single department within a hospital.
- **Front_Desk_Officer**: A staff member at the hospital's record/reception department responsible for capturing patient data at registration.
- **Patient_Record**: The structured data object representing a patient, including demographics, photographs, documents, clinical notes, and payment status.
- **Patient_Journey**: The ordered sequence of department visits a patient undergoes from registration to discharge or follow-up.
- **Doctor**: A licensed medical practitioner who reviews patient records, makes clinical decisions, and routes patients to the next care step.
- **Pharmacist**: A staff member in the Pharmacy department who receives and fulfills medication orders.
- **Lab_Technician**: A staff member in the Laboratory department who receives and processes diagnostic orders.
- **Appointment**: A scheduled future consultation between a patient and a doctor.
- **Tenant**: A single hospital instance within the multi-tenant architecture, with isolated data and configuration.
- **API_Gateway**: The Laravel backend layer that authenticates requests and routes them to the correct tenant context.
- **Mobile_App**: The Flutter-based application used by department-level staff on mobile devices.
- **Web_Dashboard**: The browser-based interface (Vue.js 3 + Inertia.js) used by Hospital_Admins and the Super_Admin, served from the Laravel application.
- **Notification_Service**: The system component responsible for delivering real-time alerts and workflow events to relevant staff, using Firebase FCM as the push notification channel for backgrounded mobile devices.
- **Audit_Log**: An immutable record of all significant system actions, including who performed them and when.
- **Invoice**: An itemised bill generated by the Accounts_Department for services rendered or to be rendered to a patient, containing one or more Invoice_Items.
- **Invoice_Item**: A single line item on an Invoice representing a specific service charge (e.g., registration fee, consultation fee, lab test fee), with a service name, unit price, quantity, and subtotal.
- **HMO**: Health Maintenance Organisation — an insurance provider whose policy/member details are captured at front-desk registration and may be used as a payment type.
- **Accountant**: A staff member in the Accounts_Department responsible for creating Invoices and validating all payments for patient visits.
- **Accounts_Department**: The hospital unit responsible for all financial transactions, Invoice creation, and payment validation.
- **PENDING_PAYMENT**: A Patient_Record status indicating that the patient has been registered but payment has not yet been confirmed; records in this state are in the Accounts_Department queue and are not visible to any clinical department queue.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: Multi-Tenant Hospital Isolation

**User Story:** As a Super_Admin, I want each hospital to operate in a fully isolated tenant environment, so that patient data, configurations, and staff accounts from one hospital are never accessible to another hospital.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE HHMS SHALL provision a separate tenant context (isolated database schema or database) for each hospital upon onboarding.
2. WHEN a user authenticates, THE API_Gateway SHALL resolve the correct tenant context before processing any request.
3. WHILE a session is active, THE API_Gateway SHALL restrict all data queries to the authenticated user's tenant context.
4. IF a request attempts to access data outside the authenticated tenant context, THEN THE API_Gateway SHALL reject the request with an HTTP 403 response and log the attempt to the Audit_Log.
5. THE HHMS SHALL ensure that tenant provisioning, suspension, and deletion are performed exclusively by the Super_Admin.

---

### Requirement 2: Super Admin System Management

**User Story:** As a Super_Admin, I want a web dashboard to manage all hospitals, system-wide configurations, and user accounts across the entire platform, so that I can oversee and control every aspect of the HHMS.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Super_Admin SHALL have access to a Web_Dashboard that lists all registered hospitals with their status (active, suspended, inactive).
2. WHEN the Super_Admin creates a new hospital record, THE HHMS SHALL provision a new Tenant, generate default Hospital_Admin credentials, and send an onboarding notification to the provided email address.
3. WHEN the Super_Admin suspends a hospital, THE HHMS SHALL immediately revoke all active sessions for that hospital's users and prevent new logins until the suspension is lifted.
4. THE Super_Admin SHALL be able to view Audit_Logs for any hospital or system-wide event, filterable by date range, hospital, and action type.
5. THE Super_Admin SHALL be able to configure system-wide settings including Mobile_App version enforcement, feature flags, and API rate limits.
6. WHERE a feature flag is disabled by the Super_Admin, THE HHMS SHALL hide the corresponding feature from all Hospital_Admin and department-level interfaces without requiring a redeployment.
7. THE Super_Admin SHALL be able to reset credentials for any Hospital_Admin account across all tenants.

---

### Requirement 3: Hospital Admin Management

**User Story:** As a Hospital_Admin, I want a web dashboard to manage my hospital's departments, staff accounts, and configurations, so that I can keep the hospital's operations running correctly within the system.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Hospital_Admin SHALL have access to a Web_Dashboard scoped exclusively to their hospital's tenant.
2. THE Hospital_Admin SHALL be able to create, update, deactivate, and assign roles to staff accounts within their hospital.
3. WHEN a Hospital_Admin creates a Department, THE HHMS SHALL generate a unique department identifier and make the department available for patient routing within that hospital.
4. THE Hospital_Admin SHALL be able to configure the patient journey workflow by defining the ordered sequence of departments a patient passes through.
5. WHEN a staff account is deactivated by the Hospital_Admin, THE HHMS SHALL immediately revoke that account's active sessions.
6. THE Hospital_Admin SHALL be able to view department-level activity reports including patient throughput, average wait times, and pending patient counts, filterable by date range and department.
7. IF a Hospital_Admin attempts to delete a Department that has active Patient_Records in progress, THEN THE HHMS SHALL reject the deletion and return a descriptive error message listing the count of active records.
8. THE Hospital_Admin SHALL be able to select a department type from the following enumerated values when creating a Department: Front Desk, Triage/Nurse, Doctor, Pharmacy, Laboratory, Ward/Admission, ICU/A&E, Accounts, and any custom type defined by the Hospital_Admin.
9. THE Hospital_Admin SHALL be able to view financial reports including total revenue by date range, outstanding Invoices, and payment breakdown by type (cash, POS/card, HMO/insurance).

---

### Requirement 4: Department Dashboard

**User Story:** As a department staff member, I want a dashboard scoped to my department, so that I can view and act on only the patients currently assigned to my unit.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Department_Dashboard SHALL display only the Patient_Records currently routed to the authenticated user's department.
2. WHEN a Patient_Record is routed to a department, THE Notification_Service SHALL deliver a real-time alert to all active staff sessions in that department within 5 seconds.
3. WHILE a Patient_Record is open in a department, THE Department_Dashboard SHALL display the patient's full journey history including all prior department notes and actions.
4. THE Department_Dashboard SHALL be accessible via both the Web_Dashboard and the Mobile_App, with feature parity for core patient record actions.
5. WHEN a staff member marks a Patient_Record as complete for their department, THE HHMS SHALL route the record to the next department in the configured patient journey workflow.

---

### Requirement 5: Patient Registration and Data Capture

**User Story:** As a Front_Desk_Officer, I want to capture complete patient information including photographs, documents, chief complaint, and insurance/HMO details at registration, so that a comprehensive Patient_Record is available to all subsequent departments.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Mobile_App SHALL provide a patient registration form that captures: full name, date of birth, gender, contact phone number, residential address, next-of-kin name and contact, chief complaint, insurance/HMO provider name, and insurance/HMO policy or member number.
2. WHEN a Front_Desk_Officer submits a registration form, THE HHMS SHALL validate that full name, date of birth, gender, contact phone number, and chief complaint are present before creating the Patient_Record.
3. IF any required field is missing during registration, THEN THE Mobile_App SHALL display a field-level validation error identifying each missing field before submission is attempted.
4. THE Mobile_App SHALL allow the Front_Desk_Officer to capture a patient photograph using the device camera or by selecting an image from the device gallery.
5. THE Mobile_App SHALL allow the Front_Desk_Officer to attach supporting documents (PDF or image files up to 10 MB each) to a Patient_Record.
6. WHEN a Patient_Record is successfully created, THE HHMS SHALL assign a unique, human-readable Patient ID, set the Patient_Record status to PENDING_PAYMENT, and route the record to the Accounts_Department queue.
7. THE HHMS SHALL store patient photographs and documents in a secure, tenant-isolated Cloudflare R2 storage bucket and SHALL NOT expose direct public URLs to stored files.
8. WHEN the Mobile_App is operating without an internet connection, THE Mobile_App SHALL queue registration data locally and synchronise with the HHMS within 60 seconds of connectivity being restored.

---

### Requirement 6: Patient Journey Workflow

**User Story:** As a clinical staff member, I want the system to route a patient's record automatically to the next department after I complete my step, so that the patient progresses through care without manual coordination.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE HHMS SHALL route a Patient_Record to the next department in the configured workflow sequence immediately upon the current department marking the record as complete.
2. WHEN a Patient_Record is routed to a new department, THE HHMS SHALL append a timestamped workflow event to the Patient_Record's journey history.
3. THE HHMS SHALL allow any department staff member to add structured notes to a Patient_Record while it is assigned to their department.
4. WHILE a Patient_Record is in transit between departments, THE HHMS SHALL display the record's status as "In Transit" and prevent concurrent editing by multiple departments.
5. IF a department staff member attempts to edit a Patient_Record that is not currently assigned to their department, THEN THE HHMS SHALL reject the edit and return a descriptive error message.
6. THE HHMS SHALL maintain a complete, immutable journey history for every Patient_Record from registration to discharge or follow-up.

---

### Requirement 7: Doctor Dashboard and Clinical Actions

**User Story:** As a Doctor, I want a dashboard where I can review a patient's full record, make clinical decisions, and route the patient to the appropriate next step, so that I can deliver care efficiently.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Department_Dashboard SHALL present the Doctor with the patient's full record including registration data, photographs, documents, and all prior department notes.
2. WHEN a Doctor selects a patient, THE Department_Dashboard SHALL display the patient's journey history in chronological order with department names, timestamps, and staff notes.
3. THE Doctor SHALL be able to route a Patient_Record to the Pharmacy department, the Laboratory department, or mark the patient for inpatient admission from the Department_Dashboard.
4. WHEN a Doctor routes a Patient_Record to the Pharmacy department, THE HHMS SHALL attach the Doctor's prescription notes to the record before routing.
5. WHEN a Doctor routes a Patient_Record to the Laboratory department, THE HHMS SHALL attach the Doctor's investigation request notes to the record before routing.
6. WHEN a Doctor marks a patient for inpatient admission, THE HHMS SHALL update the Patient_Record status to "Admitted" and notify the Hospital_Admin.
7. THE Doctor SHALL be able to book a follow-up Appointment for a patient by selecting a future date and time from the Department_Dashboard.
8. WHEN an Appointment is booked, THE HHMS SHALL store the Appointment linked to the Patient_Record and send a confirmation notification to the patient's registered contact phone number via SMS.
9. IF a Doctor attempts to book an Appointment for a date in the past, THEN THE HHMS SHALL reject the booking and display a validation error.

---

### Requirement 8: Pharmacy Department Workflow

**User Story:** As a Pharmacist, I want to receive routed patient records with the doctor's prescription, so that I can fulfil medication orders accurately and record dispensing.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Patient_Record is routed to the Pharmacy department, THE Department_Dashboard SHALL display the Doctor's prescription notes prominently alongside the patient's registration data.
2. THE Pharmacist SHALL be able to record the medications dispensed against a Patient_Record, including drug name, dosage, quantity, and dispensing timestamp.
3. WHEN the Pharmacist marks dispensing as complete, THE HHMS SHALL update the Patient_Record status and route the record according to the next step in the workflow (discharge or further routing).
4. IF a Patient_Record arrives at the Pharmacy department without prescription notes, THEN THE HHMS SHALL display a warning to the Pharmacist and log the anomaly to the Audit_Log.

---

### Requirement 9: Laboratory Department Workflow

**User Story:** As a Lab_Technician, I want to receive routed patient records with the doctor's investigation request, so that I can process tests and record results accurately.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN a Patient_Record is routed to the Laboratory department, THE Department_Dashboard SHALL display the Doctor's investigation request notes prominently alongside the patient's registration data.
2. THE Lab_Technician SHALL be able to record test results against a Patient_Record, including test name, result value, reference range, and result timestamp.
3. THE Lab_Technician SHALL be able to attach result documents (PDF or image files up to 10 MB each) to a Patient_Record.
4. WHEN the Lab_Technician marks results as complete, THE HHMS SHALL route the updated Patient_Record back to the originating Doctor's department queue and deliver a real-time alert to the Doctor.
5. IF a Patient_Record arrives at the Laboratory department without investigation request notes, THEN THE HHMS SHALL display a warning to the Lab_Technician and log the anomaly to the Audit_Log.

---

### Requirement 10: Authentication and Role-Based Access Control

**User Story:** As a system operator, I want every user to be authenticated and authorised based on their role, so that access to patient data and system functions is strictly controlled.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE HHMS SHALL authenticate all users via a token-based mechanism (JWT) before granting access to any protected resource.
2. WHEN a user's session token expires, THE HHMS SHALL require re-authentication before processing further requests.
3. THE HHMS SHALL enforce role-based access control with the following roles: Super_Admin, Hospital_Admin, Doctor, Front_Desk_Officer, Accountant, Pharmacist, Lab_Technician, and Department_Staff.
4. WHILE a user is authenticated, THE HHMS SHALL restrict API responses to data permitted by the user's assigned role and tenant context.
5. IF a user submits a request for a resource outside their role's permissions, THEN THE HHMS SHALL return an HTTP 403 response and log the attempt to the Audit_Log.
6. THE HHMS SHALL enforce a minimum password length of 10 characters and require at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one digit, and one special character.
7. WHEN a user fails authentication 5 consecutive times within a 10-minute window, THE HHMS SHALL lock the account and notify the Hospital_Admin (or Super_Admin for Hospital_Admin accounts).

---

### Requirement 11: Notifications and Alerts

**User Story:** As a staff member, I want to receive real-time notifications when a patient record is routed to my department or when an action requires my attention, so that I can respond promptly.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Notification_Service SHALL deliver in-app push notifications to the Mobile_App and Web_Dashboard for all patient routing events within 5 seconds of the event occurring.
2. WHEN an Appointment is due within 24 hours, THE Notification_Service SHALL send an SMS reminder to the patient's registered contact phone number.
3. THE Notification_Service SHALL send an email notification to a Hospital_Admin when a staff account is locked due to failed authentication attempts.
4. IF the Notification_Service fails to deliver a notification after 3 retry attempts, THEN THE HHMS SHALL log the failure to the Audit_Log and mark the notification as undelivered.
5. THE HHMS SHALL allow a Hospital_Admin to configure which notification types are enabled for their hospital.

---

### Requirement 12: Audit Logging

**User Story:** As a Hospital_Admin or Super_Admin, I want an immutable audit log of all significant system actions, so that I can investigate incidents and demonstrate compliance.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE HHMS SHALL record an Audit_Log entry for every create, update, delete, and route action performed on a Patient_Record, including the acting user's ID, role, timestamp, and action description.
2. THE HHMS SHALL record an Audit_Log entry for every authentication event (success, failure, lockout) including the user ID, IP address, and timestamp.
3. THE Audit_Log SHALL be immutable — no user role, including Super_Admin, SHALL be able to modify or delete Audit_Log entries.
4. THE Super_Admin and Hospital_Admin SHALL be able to export Audit_Log entries for their respective scope as a CSV file, filterable by date range, user, and action type.
5. THE HHMS SHALL retain Audit_Log entries for a minimum of 7 years.

---

### Requirement 13: Offline Support for Mobile App

**User Story:** As a Front_Desk_Officer or department staff member, I want the Mobile_App to remain functional when internet connectivity is intermittent, so that patient data capture is not interrupted.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHEN the Mobile_App detects loss of internet connectivity, THE Mobile_App SHALL display a clear offline status indicator to the user.
2. WHILE the Mobile_App is offline, THE Mobile_App SHALL allow staff to complete patient registration forms and queue the data locally on the device.
3. WHEN internet connectivity is restored, THE Mobile_App SHALL automatically synchronise all queued data with the HHMS within 60 seconds.
4. IF a data conflict is detected during synchronisation (the same Patient_Record was modified both locally and on the server), THEN THE Mobile_App SHALL flag the conflict for manual resolution by the Hospital_Admin.
5. THE Mobile_App SHALL encrypt all locally queued patient data using AES-256 encryption while stored on the device.

---

### Requirement 14: Performance and Scalability

**User Story:** As a Super_Admin, I want the system to handle concurrent usage across all hospitals without degradation, so that clinical workflows are never delayed by system performance.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE API_Gateway SHALL respond to 95% of authenticated API requests within 500 milliseconds under a load of 1,000 concurrent users per tenant.
2. THE HHMS SHALL support horizontal scaling of the API_Gateway and background worker processes without requiring downtime.
3. WHEN a new Tenant is provisioned, THE HHMS SHALL complete the provisioning process within 60 seconds.
4. THE HHMS SHALL implement database query caching for read-heavy endpoints (department dashboards, patient lists) with a cache TTL of 30 seconds.
5. THE Mobile_App SHALL load the department patient list within 3 seconds on a 4G mobile connection.

---

### Requirement 15: Data Security and Privacy

**User Story:** As a Hospital_Admin, I want patient data to be encrypted and access-controlled, so that the hospital meets healthcare data protection obligations.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE HHMS SHALL encrypt all patient data at rest using AES-256 encryption.
2. THE HHMS SHALL enforce HTTPS (TLS 1.2 or higher) for all data in transit between clients and the API_Gateway.
3. THE HHMS SHALL store patient photographs and documents in a tenant-isolated storage bucket and generate time-limited signed URLs (valid for no more than 15 minutes) for authorised access.
4. THE HHMS SHALL allow a Hospital_Admin to request permanent deletion of a patient's personal data in compliance with applicable data protection regulations, subject to retention obligations.
5. IF a Hospital_Admin requests patient data deletion, THEN THE HHMS SHALL anonymise the Patient_Record by replacing personally identifiable fields with placeholder values while retaining the clinical and audit history.

---

### Requirement 16: Accounts Department and Payment Management

**User Story:** As an Accountant, I want to create payment Invoices and validate all payments for patient visits, so that the hospital collects fees before clinical services are delivered.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE HHMS SHALL route every new Patient_Record with status PENDING_PAYMENT to the Accounts_Department queue immediately after registration.
2. THE Accountant SHALL be able to create an Invoice for a Patient_Record containing one or more Invoice_Items, each with a service name, unit price, quantity, and subtotal.
3. WHEN creating an Invoice for a new patient, THE HHMS SHALL require Invoice_Items for both the registration/card fee and the consultation fee.
4. WHEN creating an Invoice for a returning patient, THE HHMS SHALL require an Invoice_Item for the consultation fee only.
5. THE Accountant SHALL be able to record payment against an Invoice by specifying: payment type (cash, POS/card, HMO/insurance), amount paid, and receipt or transaction reference.
6. WHEN the full Invoice amount is paid and the Accountant marks the Invoice as settled, THE HHMS SHALL update the Patient_Record status from PENDING_PAYMENT to active and route the record to the first clinical department in the configured workflow.
7. WHILE a Patient_Record has PENDING_PAYMENT status, THE Department_Dashboard SHALL exclude that record from all clinical department queues (Triage, Doctor, Pharmacy, Lab, Ward, ICU/A&E).
8. IF an Accountant attempts to mark an Invoice as settled when the amount paid is less than the Invoice total, THEN THE HHMS SHALL reject the action and display a descriptive error showing the outstanding balance.
9. WHERE a patient's payment type is HMO/insurance, THE Accountant SHALL be able to record the HMO provider name, policy/member number, and claim reference number on the Invoice.
10. THE Accountant SHALL be able to create additional Invoices for a Patient_Record at any point during the visit (e.g., for lab tests or pharmacy items ordered by the Doctor).
11. THE Hospital_Admin SHALL be able to view financial reports including: total revenue by date range, outstanding Invoices, payment breakdown by type (cash/POS/HMO), and revenue by department/service.
12. THE HHMS SHALL record an Audit_Log entry for every Invoice creation, payment recording, and Invoice settlement, including the Accountant's user ID, Invoice ID, amount, and timestamp.
13. AN Invoice SHALL be immutable once marked as settled; IF a correction is required, THEN the Hospital_Admin SHALL authorise a reversal entry recorded as a separate Audit_Log event.
14. THE Accountant SHALL be able to generate and print/export a receipt for any settled Invoice.
